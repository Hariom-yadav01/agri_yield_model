# Model Card: Vidarbha District-Crop Yield Forecaster

**Status: demonstration model trained on SYNTHETIC data. Not validated on real farms. Retrain on real records before any operational use.**

## 1. Overview
- **Purpose:** forecast crop yield (quintal/ha) for plots in six districts (Nagpur, Wardha, Amravati, Yavatmal, Chandrapur, Bhandara) early enough to guide procurement, and flag block-crop-seasons likely to fall short.
- **Model:** LightGBM regressor (500 trees, 127 leaves, L1 objective, learning rate 0.03) predicting `log(yield / 3-year village-crop mean)`, then multiplied back. Per district-crop recalibration factors are stored in the bundle.
- **File:** `outputs/yield_model.joblib` (dict with model, 53 feature names, recalibration table, shortfall threshold). Scoring helper: `predict_yield.py`.
- **Forecast origin:** kharif crops (cotton, soybean, tur, rice) at 30 Sep; rabi crops (wheat, gram) at 31 Jan. Nothing observed after the origin is used.

## 2. Data (all simulated)
- Generated by `gen_data.py` with a fixed seed, run once. About 332,000 raw rows across yields (294k), rainfall, soil tests, mandi prices, sown area and cost indices.
- Planted problems, as in the brief: prices in Rs/quintal and Rs/kg in one column (20% of price rows in Rs/kg), 18.6% of monsoon rainfall readings missing, yields in wrong units (7.0% before cleaning).
- A ground-truth file exists and was used **only** to audit the cleaning code.
- After cleaning: 263,386 modelling rows. Remaining wrong-unit rate 0.66% by audit; 3.4% when judged by labels.

## 3. Evaluation setup
- **Time-aware split:** train 2007-2019 (190,201 rows), validation 2020-2021 (29,291), test 2022-2024 (43,894). Expanding-window CV folds inside the training years; tuning never touched validation or test years.
- **Primary metric:** MAE in quintal/ha at block-crop-year level (the unit procurement plans use). Secondary: plot-level MAE, and recall at >=70% precision for flagging a shortfall (actual yield below 85% of its trailing 3-year mean).
- **Baseline:** last year's yield (persistence).

## 4. Results

| Model | Val MAE (q/ha) | Test MAE (q/ha) | Test recall @ >=70% precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persistence (baseline) | 2.108 | 4.091 | 0.00 (cannot reach 70%) |
| 3-year mean | 2.311 | 2.996 | 0.00 |
| Ridge (per crop) | 1.994 | 2.405 | 0.63 |
| Random forest | 1.532 | 1.700 | 0.77 |
| LightGBM default | 1.538 | 1.713 | 0.80 |
| **LightGBM tuned (final)** | **1.500** | **1.623** | **0.79** (precision 0.83) |

### Success criteria
| Criterion | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Beat baseline by >=15% | 28.8% (val), 60.3% (test) | **Met** |
| Test within 5% of validation | Block-crop-year MAE +8.2%; plot-level MAE +4.5% | **Not met on the primary metric**, met at plot level |
| Top-5 drivers make business sense | irrigation, cumulative rain, rain vs crop water need, Jun-Sep rain, 3-yr block-crop yield | **Met** |

The 8.2% gap is not hidden or tuned away. Validation is only two years and test is three, and per-year MAE ranges from 1.37 to 1.78. Test years are also harder for the baseline (2023 baseline MAE 4.9), so the world drifts between periods. Adversarial validation separates the periods perfectly (AUC 1.0), which confirms drift.

### Tuning
Random search over 10 configurations. CV MAE moved from 1.433 to 1.431, a negligible gain. The better test score of the tuned model over the default (1.62 vs 1.71) should not be read as a robust improvement.

## 5. Explainability
Mean |SHAP| by group: weather 26%, ratio features 24%, rolling 15%, plot 11%, lag 8%, encoded 8%, category 5%, soil 2.5%. Top features: `irrigated_flag`, `rain_cum`, `rain_ratio_req`, `junsep`, `bc_roll3`. Directions agree with agronomy (more rain relative to crop need raises yield; irrigation buffers dry seasons). Soil features matter little here, which reflects how the simulation was built; do not generalise it.

## 6. Leakage controls
Dropped as unavailable at forecast origin: `production_qtl` (= yield x area), `insurance_claim_paid`, harvest-month price, post-cutoff rain. Including them cut MAE from 2.19 to 0.92, which is the signature of leakage. A causality test (300/300 rows unchanged when post-cutoff inputs are altered) confirms the final features use no future information.

## 7. Fairness and stability
- Plot-level relative error is similar across districts (13.7-17.5%), farm-size bands (15.4-15.6%) and data-completeness groups (14.8-15.8%). Before recalibration the model under-forecast cotton by about 9% on average and was slightly low in most districts (bias -1% to -5%); the unknown-irrigation group had the highest error (18%).
- Largest bias sits in specific district-crop-years, e.g. Wardha wheat 2023 (+37%) and Yavatmal gram 2024 (-22%): the model under-reacts to season-specific shocks.
- Recalibration by district-crop (fitted on validation) reduced mean absolute cell bias on test from 4.21% to 3.72% and left MAE flat (1.623 to 1.616).
- Robustness: 5% noise on rainfall changes forecasts by 2.5% on average; 15% noise by 7.0% (MAE 1.68). Removing gauge data raises MAE from 1.62 to 1.74.

## 8. Known wrong prediction
Yavatmal wheat, village YVT-B03-V11, 2024: forecast 20.1 q/ha, actual 37.4 (6 plots). Monsoon rainfall was missing and imputed as a 40% deficit, and the village's 3-year history was dragged down by a 10.3 q/ha year in 2023. The model anchored on that history and the imputed dry signal, while half the plots were irrigated and recovered fully. Peer villages in the district were off by only -12%, so this is a village-specific miss.

## 9. Intended use and limits
- **Use for:** ranking district-crop pairs by expected volume and shortfall risk to sequence procurement. For 2024 the plan put 14 pairs in "lock in early", 4 in "contract with backup supply", 13 in "opportunistic", 5 in "monitor"; in hindsight 32 of 36 calls were right, 3 false alarms, 1 missed shortfall.
- **Not for:** individual-farm decisions, insurance pricing, or any decision relying on exact tonnage.
- **Limits:** synthetic data; fixed forecast origins; small-n district-crop cells (e.g. cotton in Bhandara, 14-15 rows) are unreliable; pest outbreaks and local shocks are not predictable from these features.
- **Monitoring:** track error by district-crop each season, re-fit annually, and refresh recalibration factors.
